About & Mission

Justice for Every Bangladeshi

Who we are, the mission we serve, and the roadmap to 2030.

Our Mission
About A ai

Who We Are

A ai is the next-generation digital legal aid ecosystem for Bangladesh, executed by the Directorate of Bangladesh Legal Aid (DBLA) under the Ministry of Law, Justice & Parliamentary Affairs, with technical partnership from UNDP and funding from the European Union.

Our Mission

Democratize access to justice by making legal information, assistance and dispute resolution affordable, transparent and accessible to every citizen - regardless of language, literacy or economic status.

Our Vision

A Bangladesh where no one is denied justice. By 2030, autonomous AI-assisted services divert 40% of disputes to Online Dispute Resolution and reach 682,500+ vulnerable citizens.

Who Runs It

Executed by DBLA / MoLJPA, with UNDP Bangladesh as technical partner and the European Union as funding partner - governed by an ethical AI oversight board.

Vision

To democratize access to justice in Bangladesh by leveraging AI, making legal information affordable and accessible to every citizen, regardless of language, literacy, or economic status.

Mission

Build a user-centric digital platform integrating AI to bridge citizens and the legal system, empowering individuals with knowledge, tools, and connections to resolve legal issues efficiently.

The Rebel Poet

কাজী নজরুল ইসলাম - Our National Poet

24 May 1899 (11 Joishtho 1306) - 29 August 1976

"বল বীর - বল উন্নত মম শির!" — বিদ্রোহী, 1921

Nazrul with his sitar (before 1940) · Nazrul Academy · Public Domain
Wikimedia Commons · বাংলা উইকিপিডিয়া · বাংলাপিডিয়া

THE TITLES

জাতীয় কবি (National Poet) · বিদ্রোহী কবি (The Rebel Poet) · বুলবুল (The Bulbul of Bengali music)
Childhood name: দুখু মিয়া (Dukhu Mia) · Pseudonym: ধূমকেতু

EARLY LIFE

Born in Churulia, Bardhaman, West Bengal. His imam father died in 1908, leaving 9-year-old Nazrul to work as a majar caretaker, mosque muazzin, maktab teacher, bakery boy - then star of a Leto folk-theatre troupe, writing his first songs and plays.

THE REBEL

British Indian Army, 49 Bengal Regiment (1917-20). 'বিদ্রোহী' (Dec 1921) rewired Bengali poetry; the biweekly ধূমকেতু (Aug 1922) shook the empire. Charged with sedition, he answered from the dock with রাজবন্দীর জবানবন্দী and a 40-day hunger strike. Tagore dedicated his book 'Basanta' to the imprisoned poet.

THE WORK

~3,000-4,000 songs (melody & music), 3 novels, 19 short stories, 5 essay volumes, landmark poems (প্রলয়োল্লাস, কামাল পাশা, সাম্যবাদী, সর্বহারা), books (অগ্নিবীণা, বিষের বাঁশি, বাঁধন হারা). His music spans raga, ghazal, hamd, naat, Shyama Sangeet and folk - a secular synthesis in sound.

THE SILENCE & THE HONOUR

A 1942 nervous illness silenced him for 34 years. Bangladesh brought him home in 1972; D.Litt from Dhaka University (1975); citizenship and Ekushe Padak (1976). He died 29 August 1976 and rests beside the DU central mosque. Independence Award, 1977.

WHY HE MATTERS HERE

His poetry's core was protest against the oppression of man by man - equality, religious tolerance, women's emancipation: the same justice A ai serves. His final words: "আমি শুধু হিন্দু মুসলিমকে এক জায়গায় ধরে নিয়ে হ্যান্ডশেক করানোর চেষ্টা করেছি।"

His Dhaka mausoleum is also the resting place of Shaheed Sharif Osman Bin Hadi · see the memorial
