The Spark of Resistance

Shaheed Abrar Fahad

আবরার ফাহাদ রাব্বি · 12 February 1998 - 7 October 2019

"His question still echoes: why must we give our own to light another's lamp?"

Photo: banglanews24.com, via Bengali Wikipedia (fair use - memorial/educational)
বাংলা উইকিপিডিয়া - আবরার ফাহাদ

FULL NAME

Abrar Fahad Rabbi (আবরার ফাহাদ রাব্বি) - known as Rabbi to family

BORN

12 February 1998, Kushtia - village home Kumarkhali

FAMILY

Father: Barkatullah (BRAC inspection officer)
Mother: Rokaya Khatun (kindergarten teacher)
Younger brother: Abrar Fayaz - admitted to BUET Mechanical, 2022

EDUCATION

Kushtia Mission Primary → Kushtia Zilla School → Notre Dame College (Science)
BUET, Dept. of Electrical & Electronic Engineering (2018, 2nd year)

6-7 OCTOBER 2019

Returned to Sher-e-Bangla Hall for exams. That night, in room 2011, at least 20 Chhatra League leaders beat him for hours over his Facebook posts criticizing Bangladesh-India deals. Pronounced dead at 3:00 AM, 7 October. Autopsy: blunt-force beating.

RESTING PLACE

Kushtia

His final Facebook post

Hours before his death, Abrar questioned three India-Bangladesh deals: giving India access to the Mongla and Chittagong ports; granting India Feni river water while Indian states refuse sharing among themselves; and importing LNG when gas shortages idle our own factories. He closed with Tagore's couplet:

"পরের কারণে স্বার্থ দিয়া বলি
এ জীবন মন সকলি দাও,
তার মত সুখ কোথাও কি আছে
আপনার কথা ভুলিয়া যাও।"

That post - and that question - made him a symbol. The full justice timeline, his legacy, and the response his death sparked are below - his killing seeded the resistance that grew into the July 2024 uprising.

The road to justice

7 Oct 2019, 3:00 AM

Pronounced dead at Sher-e-Bangla Hall ground floor; CCTV had recorded him being dragged down the corridor hours earlier. His father Barkatullah files a murder case against 19 at Chawkbazar police station.

8-15 Oct 2019

BUET students rise with 10 demands - maximum punishment, lifetime expulsion of killers, ban on campus student politics, CCTV in every hall. Protests spread to Dhaka University, Jahangirnagar, Islamic University, Khulna, Rajshahi, SUST and beyond; human chains in 13+ districts.

22 Oct 2019

BUET administration lifetime-expels 26 students over the killing, bans organizational student politics on campus, promises case costs, compensation, CCTV and an online complaint platform.

13 Nov 2019

Detective Branch submits the chargesheet: 25 accused. The case goes to the speediest tribunal with a 135-day trial clock.

7 Dec 2021

Judge Abu Jafar Kamruzzaman (Dhaka 1st Speediest Tribunal): 20 sentenced to death - including BUET Chhatra League general secretary Mehedee Hasan Russel and information secretary Onik Sarker - and 5 to life imprisonment. Three remain fugitives: Morsheduzzaman Jisan, Ehteshamul Rabbi Tanim, Mostoba Rafid. The court finds the accused conspired and killed Fahad over fabricated Shibir allegations.

16 Mar 2025

The High Court - Justice Syed Enayet Hosain and Justice AKM Asaduzzaman - upholds 20 death sentences and 5 life terms; full verdict released 3 May 2025.

What his name became

Independence Award 2025

Bangladesh's highest civilian honor, posthumous - in the new "Rebellious Youth" (প্রতিবাদী তারুণ্য) category, conferred 25 March 2025.

Shaheed Abrar Fahad Avenue

Dhaka's Bangabandhu Avenue renamed for him on 25 March 2025.

Shaheed Abrar Fahad Stadium

Kushtia Stadium renamed, 2025 - in the district where he was born.

A national day

7 October, the day he was killed, declared a national day of remembrance (October 2025), alongside the Eight Pillars Against Aggression monument at Palashi intersection and his memorial plaque at Sher-e-Bangla Hall.

Room Number 2011

A short film (October 2024) named for the hall room where he was killed, on his death, student politics and free expression.

In print

চেতনায় আবরার ফাহাদ (Farzana Boby Chumki, Mahakal, 2025 - unveiled at the Ekushey Book Fair), the essay collection গাজা থেকে বাংলাদেশ, and Mita Ali's novel আকর (2025).

The response his death sparked

On campus

The 10-point movement forced real change: lifetime expulsions, the campus ban on organizational student politics, hall CCTV, and a public complaint platform - demands students had raised for years.

Across the nation

His killing ignited the deepest student movement since 2018 and planted the seeds of what grew into the July 2024 uprising. Five years later, his batchmates laid the memorial cornerstone at Palashi on his 5th death anniversary.

Around the world

The United Nations demanded a proper investigation; the US, UK, Germany and France expressed shock and pressed for justice. AFP, Reuters, BBC, Al Jazeera, the New York Times, the Washington Post and the Guardian all covered the killing - most attributing it to his criticism of Bangladesh-India deals.

Shaheed of July 18

Shaheed Mir Mugdho

মীর মাহফুজুর রহমান মুগ্ধ · 9 October 1998 - 18 July 2024

“Does anyone need water? Water, water?” — পানি লাগবে পানি

Photo: Prothom Alo, via English Wikipedia (fair use - memorial/educational)
English Wikipedia - Mir Mugdho · বাংলা উইকিপিডিয়া

FULL NAME

Mir Mahfuzur Rahman Mugdho (মীর মাহফুজুর রহমান মুগ্ধ) - known as Mugdho

BORN

9 October 1998, Uttara, Dhaka - twin brother Snigdho born one minute earlier; family from Ramrail, Brahmanbaria

FAMILY

Father: Mir Mustafizur Rahman (health inspector)
Mother: Shahana Chowdhury
Twin brother: Mir Mahbubur Rahman Snigdho (filmmaker) · Elder brother: Mir Dipto

EDUCATION

Uttara High School & College
BSc Mathematics, Khulna University (2023)
MBA (ongoing), Bangladesh University of Professionals

WORK & LIFE

Fiverr freelancer - 1,000+ projects in SEO & social media ($2,000-3,000/mo)
Bangladesh Scout, footballer, traveller

18 JULY 2024

Distributed food, water and biscuits to protesters at Azampur crossing from 4 pm. Shot in the head around 5 pm - 15 minutes after the viral "water" video. Pronounced dead at Uttara Crescent Hospital, age 25.

RESTING PLACE

Kamarpara Bamnartek Graveyard, Sector 10, Uttara, Dhaka

“পানি লাগবে পানি” - the slogan he left behind

His final video - a young man with a water case, asking over and over if anyone needs water - became the symbolic slogan of the July Uprising. "Pani lagbe pani" spread as graffiti on walls across Bangladesh, appeared as a giant water-bottle motif in the 14 April 2025 Ananda Shobhajatra, was projected in the largest drone show at Manik Mia Avenue, and "Mugdho" water bottles were handed out at a post-revolution cartoon festival. On 5 August 2025 the Mugdho Safe Drinking Water Corner opened at Joydebpur Junction railway station - free water for travellers, the very thing he died giving. Uttara's Bangabandhu Mukta Mancha was renamed Mugdho Mancha (Aug 2024), and from July 2026 his portrait appears in the watermark of Bangladesh's new e-passport (pages 32-33) alongside Abu Sayed and Wasim Akram.

His writings, his speeches and his lifelong call for an end to hegemony and fascism continue to move students and citizens across the country. That is why his contribution to Bangladesh is infinite.

Chattogram’s First Shaheed

Shaheed Wasim Akram

ওয়াসিম আকরাম · 1998 - 16 July 2024

"His result came after him: First Class - a topper’s ending, written by a martyr’s hand."

Photo: The Business Standard, via Bengali Wikipedia (fair use - memorial/educational)
বাংলা উইকিপিডিয়া - ওয়াসিম আকরাম

FULL NAME

Mohammad Wasim Akram (মোঃ ওয়াসিম আকরাম) - Chattogram division of the July Revolution

BORN

1998, Mehernama village, Pekua Upazila, Cox’s Bazar - second of five siblings

FAMILY

Father: Shafiul Alam · Mother: Jyosna Begum

EDUCATION

Mehernama High School (SSC 2017) → Bakalia Govt. College (HSC 2019) → Chattogram College, BA (Hons) English, 4th year

SHAHADAT

16 July 2024, ~4 PM, Bahaddarhat, Chattogram - shot in head and chest during Chhatra League/Jubo League attacks on students; pronounced dead at Chattogram Medical College Hospital

His last Facebook status

On 16 July 2024 he posted: চলে আসুন ষোলোশহর - "Come to Sholoshohor" - calling people to the streets of Chattogram. That afternoon he was shot.

His honours result was published 2.5 months after his death: First Class, 11th among all English department students - he never saw it.

Legacy

Thousands attended his janaza on 17 July at Pekua; buried in the family graveyard. Chattogram College students hold tree-planting and prayer events in his memory.

A Shaheed Wasim Akram Smritistambha (memorial monument) stands in his honor, and his words are painted as graffiti on the city’s expressway pillars. He is honored among the first-rank martyrs of the July Revolution, and his portrait appears in the e-passport watermark alongside Abu Sayed and Mir Mugdho.

Sources: বাংলা উইকিপিডিয়া · shibir.org.bd · Prothom Alo
The boy who kept his word

Shaheed Farhan Faiyaaz

ফারহান ফাইয়াজ · 12 September 2007 - 18 July 2024

"Build a life such that people remember you after death."

Photo: The Business Standard, via Bengali Wikipedia (fair use - memorial/educational)
বাংলা উইকিপিডিয়া - ফারহান ফাইয়াজ

FULL NAME

Mohammad Farhanul Islam Bhuiyan (মোহাম্মদ ফারহানুল ইসলাম ভূঁইয়া) - "Farhan Faiyaaz"

BORN

12 September 2007, Dhaka

FAMILY

Father: Shahidul Islam (businessman) · mother and a younger sister, Sayima

EDUCATION

Dhaka Residential Model College (DRMC) from class 3; resident student; Class XI Science, HSC batch of 2025 - aged 17

SHAHADAT

18 July 2024, Dhanmondi 27 - shot in the chest at the frontline; carried on a rickshaw, then an ambulance to City Hospital, Mohammadpur; died in treatment

The bio he wrote himself

“একদিন পৃথিবী ছেড়ে চলে যেতে হবে। এমন জীবন গড়ো, যাতে মৃত্যুর পর মানুষ তোমাকে মনে রাখে।”
"One day we must leave this world. Build a life such that people remember you after death." - from his own Facebook bio. He kept his word.

He dreamed of research, higher study abroad, and returning to build his country. His father testified as the first prosecution witness at the International Crimes Tribunal (June 2026); classmate Wasif Munim, who carried him, testified in July 2026. Formal charges were accepted against 28 accused in January 2026.

What his name became

Shaheed Farhan Faiyaaz Playground west of the National Parliament (Nov 2024) · Shaheed Farhan Faiyaz Road - Dhanmondi Road 27, renamed May 2025, the street where he fell. His college commemorates him every July.

Sources: বাংলা উইকিপিডিয়া · julyshohid.com · The Daily Star
The boy with the flag

Shaheed Golam Nafiz

গোলাম নাফিজ · 22 May 2008 - 4 August 2024

"He held the flag in death the way he held it in life."

Photo: Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0 (commemoration banner - no free portrait exists)
Wikimedia Commons - Shaheed Nafiz banner

FULL NAME

Md. Golam Nafiz (মোঃ গোলাম নাফিজ)

BORN

22 May 2008, Mahakhali, Dhaka - younger of two brothers

FAMILY

Father: Golam Ahmed · Mother: Nasima Akter

EDUCATION

Banani Bidyaniketan School & College (SSC 2024); admitted to Navy College, Dhaka - he never got to attend a single class

SHAHADAT

4 August 2024, ~4:30 PM, Farmgate - shot by police; on the rickshaw he was still alive, but Chhatra League men blocked the way; pronounced dead at hospital. Aged 17.

The photograph that crossed the country

On the rickshaw, unconscious, with the flag of Bangladesh tied around his head, hanging from the footrest - photographer Jibon Ahmed (Daily Manab Zamin) captured the image that ran on the paper’s front page and became one of the uprising’s defining pictures.

Rickshaw puller Noor Mohammad peddled through the blockade trying to save him; the rickshaw itself is now preserved at the July Revolution Memorial Museum. Banani Bidyaniketan named a building after him. His father spent the whole night searching hospital to hospital, finding him only the next morning at the Suhrawardy morgue.

Voices Silenced · Justice Demanded

Victims of Injustice

Three lives that shook Bangladesh — each taken by power, each demanding the same thing: justice. Their names became roads, symbols and rallying cries.

2011
Killed by BSF · Kurigram Border
ফেলানি খাতুন
Killed by BSF · Kurigram Border

Felani Khatun

c. 1996 – 7 Jan 2011

A 15-year-old girl shot dead by Indian BSF at dawn on the border fence as she returned to Bangladesh — her body left hanging on the wire for hours. The image shook the nation and became the enduring symbol of border-killing impunity that the uprising would later demand be ended.

Justice: Killer acquitted twice by BSF court-martialLegacy: Felani Avenue, Diplomatic Zone, Dhaka (2025)
2019
Killed by Chhatra League · BUET
আবরার ফাহাদ
Killed by Chhatra League · BUET

Abrar Fahad

12 Feb 1998 – 7 Oct 2019

A BUET student beaten to death with cricket stumps for a Facebook post opposing Indian influence. His murder ignited a nationwide student movement — and became a founding wound of the anti-fascist struggle the 2024 uprising finished.

Verdict: 20 death · 5 life (2021), upheld 2025Legacy: Shaheed Abrar Fahad Avenue · Independence Award 2025

Sharif Osman Bin Hadi
Shot in Paltan · National Martyr

1 Jan 1993 – 18 Dec 2025

July Uprising 2024 coordinator and Inqilab Moncho co-founder & spokesperson, shot in Paltan and airlifted to Singapore, where he died. His state funeral at the Sangsad and burial beside Kazi Nazrul Islam made him the nation’s newest symbol of the unfinished fight for justice.
