Constitution of Bangladesh

The Supreme Law of the Land

Adopted 4 November 1972, effective 16 December 1972. 153 Articles across 11 Parts. 17 Amendments.

Fundamental Principles

All 11 Parts of the Constitution

Part I: The Republic

Art 1-6. Bangladesh is a unitary people's republic. Bengali is the official language. Fundamental principles: nationalism, socialism, democracy, secularism.

Art 1-6RepublicBengali

Part II: Fundamental Principles

Art 7-8. Constitution is supreme law. Basic structure doctrine. 4 principles + human rights. No amendment can destroy basic structure.

Art 7-8SupremacyBasic Structure

Part III: Fundamental Rights

Art 26-47. Equality, life and liberty, freedom of movement, speech, religion, assembly. Writ jurisdiction (Art 44). 6 writs available.

Art 26-47RightsWrits

Part IV: The Executive

Art 48-65. President (Head of State). PM (Head of Govt). Cabinet. President acts on PM advice. No PM term limit.

Art 48-65PresidentPrime Minister

Part V: The Legislature

Art 65-95. Jatiya Sangsad: 300 elected + 50 women seats. Speaker. Quorum 60. 5yr term. Art 70 party discipline.

Art 65-95Parliament350 Seats

Part VI: The Judiciary

Art 94-116. Supreme Court: Appellate + High Court. Judges retire at 67. Separation from executive. Art 111 precedent.

Art 94-116Supreme CourtIndependence

Part VII: Elections

Art 117-122. Election Commission. CEC appointed by President. Independent body for all elections.

Art 117-122ElectionsCommission

Part VIII: C&AG

Art 123-125. Comptroller & Auditor-General. Audits all public accounts. Reports to Parliament.

Art 123-125AuditIndependence

Part IX: The Services

Art 126-141. Public Service Commission. BCS recruitment. 26 cadre services. Retirement 59.

Art 126-141BCSCivil Service

Part X: Amendment

Art 142. Requires 2/3 majority of total MPs. Cannot alter basic features. SC reviews amendments.

Art 1422/3 MajorityBasic Structure

Part XI: Miscellaneous

Art 142-153. Official language, transitional provisions, short title, enactment of laws, final provisions.

Art 142-153TransitionalFinal

Art 27 - Equality

All citizens equal before law. Equal protection. No discrimination.

Art 27

Art 32 - Right to Life

No deprivation of life or liberty except by law. Inviolable right.

Art 32

Art 39 - Free Speech

Freedom of thought, conscience, speech, press. Reasonable restrictions.

Art 39

Key Constitutional Bodies

President

Head of State (Art 48). Elected by Parliament. 5yr term, max 2.

Prime Minister

Head of Govt (Art 56). Leader of majority. Executive in PM name.

Chief Justice

Heads Supreme Court (Art 94). Appellate + HC. Judicial independence.

C&AG

Audits all public accounts (Art 123). Reports to Parliament.

Election Commission

All elections (Art 117). CEC + Commissioners. Independent.

Public Service Commission

BCS recruitment (Art 126). 26 cadre services. Merit-based.
