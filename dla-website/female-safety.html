Know Your Shield

Women Protection Laws of Bangladesh নারী সুরক্ষা আইন

Your legal rights are your permanent shield. Every woman deserves to know them.

⚖️ The Domestic Violence (Prevention & Protection) Act 2010

Protection orders, residence orders and monetary relief for victims. Harassment, dowry demand, and emotional abuse are covered. Complaint to: Women & Children Repression Tribunal, OSCC, or 109.

Helpline: 109 (Women Support)

👩‍👩 The Prevention of Women & Children Repression Act 2000

Stringent punishments for rape, acid violence, trafficking and sexual harassment - including death penalty for the most serious offences. Trial in special tribunals in every district.

24/7 Special Tribunals in every district

💰 The Dowry Prohibition Act 1980

Demanding dowry is a criminal offence punishable by up to 5 years imprisonment plus fine. Givers, takers and abettors are all liable.

Report to: 999 or nearest OC office

💻 Cyber Security Act 2023

Protects women from online harassment, morphing, blackmail and cyber stalking. Sharing intimate images without consent carries long imprisonment.

Cyber Helpline: 333 · DMP Cyber Unit

🎓 The Acid Crime Prevention Act 2002

Acid attacks carry life imprisonment; sale of acid is regulated. Victims get free medical treatment and compensation from the Acid Survivors Foundation.

Free treatment: Acid Survivors Foundation

💼 Constitution of Bangladesh - Art 27, 28, 29

All citizens are equal before the law (Art 27). Women have equal rights in state and public life (Art 28), with special provisions for advancement (Art 29).

Supreme Law of the Land

👩‍💻 Digital Security / ICT Act on Revenge Porn

Morphing photos, non-consensual intimate image sharing and online blackmail are specific offences with severe sentences.

Report: DMP Cyber Support for Women

🏥️ Labour Act 2006 (Workplace Safety)

Sexual harassment at work violates the High Court guidelines and Labour Act. Employers must form internal complaints committees.

Report: Labour Court / 16430
