শহীদ শরিফ ওসমান বিন হাদি
the nation's immortal son of the July Revolution

From the frontlines of the 2024 July Uprising to the founding of Inqilab Moncho, Hadi gave the nation its conscience — demanding the revolution's full justice until his last breath. His name now stands honoured with the martyrs of the uprising on the July 2024 memorial page: in the "Honouring the Martyrs" strip, the gallery of the revolution, and the official list of martyrs. Writer, teacher, coordinator & national martyr — his contribution to Bangladesh is infinite.

His Journey

A life devoted to justice

1993

Born in Harikhali village, Nalchity, Jhalakathi district.

2010–11

Admitted to the University of Dhaka, Department of Political Science.

Teacher

Lecturer at the University of Scholars, Dhaka — writer and cultural organiser.

July 2024

Key coordinator of the student uprising that toppled the autocratic regime on 5 August 2024.

2025

Co-founded Inqilab Moncho and became its spokesperson — voice for full justice of the revolution.

Dec 2025

Shot on 12 December in Paltan, Dhaka; airlifted to Singapore, where he died on 18 December 2025.

A nation remembers its son

The Chief Adviser

“Hadi, you are in our hearts forever.”

Statement issued on behalf of the Chief Adviser of Bangladesh following his death.

The Nation’s Farewell

A state janaza was held on 20 December 2025 at the South Plaza of the Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban, with mourners lining the streets of Dhaka. He was laid to rest beside Kazi Nazrul Islam at the Dhaka University central mosque mausoleum.

Vigils and processions spread across the country’s districts demanding justice.

His Legacy

Roads and public spaces have been named after him, and his writings, speeches and calls for an end to hegemony continue to move a generation of students and citizens.

National martyr of the July Uprising 2024.
